Privacy policy: Zenith Account Switcher

Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: your saved logins stay on your own computer. The extension never sends your cookies, the websites you use or your browsing to us or to anyone else. It has no analytics and no ads.

Who we are

Zenith Account Switcher is made and sold by Zenith, a small software business in India (zenitherps.com). Contact: info@zenitherps.com.

What the extension handles

  • Login data for websites you choose. When you press Save this account on a website, the extension copies that website's cookies and local storage, so it can put them back when you switch to that account.
  • What you type into the extension. Account names, colours, folders and notes.
  • Settings. For example your language, theme, auto-lock time and whether a passphrase is set.
  • Your licence key, if you buy Pro.

The extension never sees or stores your passwords. It only touches a website after you choose to save an account there and Chrome asks you to allow access to that one website.

Where it is kept

  • Only on your computer, in the extension's own storage inside Chrome. It is not synced to your Google account or to any cloud.
  • Saved logins are encrypted. If you set a passphrase, they can only be opened after you type it; the passphrase itself is never stored.
  • If you export a backup file, that file contains your saved logins (encrypted with the backup password you choose). Keep it somewhere safe.

What is sent over the internet

Only one thing: when you enter a Pro licence key, and from time to time afterwards, the extension asks our licence server (licence.zenitherps.com) whether the key is valid. It sends the key and the product name, nothing else. No cookies, no website names, no browsing activity, no account names.

The extension loads no code from the internet. All its code is inside the package you install from the Chrome Web Store.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share your data.
  • We do not use it for advertising, credit checks or any purpose other than the extension's single purpose: saving your own logins and switching between them.
  • We do not track you. There are no analytics inside the extension.

The use of information received from the extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

When you buy Pro

Payment happens on our website (zenitherps.com/buy), not inside the extension. Payments are taken by PayPal, or by UPI for buyers in India. We never see your card details. To issue your licence and help you later, we keep your email address, your licence key, the plan, the amount and the payment reference. We use these only to send you your key, to answer your questions and to handle refunds. See the refund policy and the terms of sale.

Your control

  • Delete one saved account, or everything, from the extension at any time.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored on your computer.
  • You can take back website access in Chrome under Extensions > Zenith Account Switcher > Details > Site access.
  • To ask what we hold about your purchase, or to have it deleted, email info@zenitherps.com. We keep payment records only as long as the law requires.

Children

The extension is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your computer, we will say so in the extension before it applies.
