Privacy policy: zenitherps.com

Last updated 25 November 2026

In short: Google Analytics runs only if you press Accept. Error reports are sent without cookies and without your name, email or licence key. There are no ads and we do not sell data.

This page covers the website and the licence server. The Chrome extensions have their own policies: Zenith Account Switcher and Zenith Timesheet.

Who we are

Zenith is a small software business in India (zenitherps.com). Contact: info@zenitherps.com.

Google Analytics, only if you accept

If you press Accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (names starting with _ga) and tells us which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from, what kind of device they use, and actions such as tapping Call or WhatsApp, reading a section, sending a form, or the steps of a purchase (product, plan, price and the PayPal order number). It does not get your name, email, phone number, licence key or what you type in a form. Google's advertising features are off.

If you press Decline, or choose nothing, Google Analytics is not loaded. Your choice is kept in your own browser. To change it, press Cookie settings at the bottom of any page; declining also deletes the Google Analytics cookies.

Error reports

When something on the website or the licence server fails, a report goes to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA) so we can fix it. From the website it has the error, the page address without its query part, the time, and your browser's name and version. From the licence server it has the error, the time and, if a payment was involved, the PayPal order or payment number. It uses no cookies. We blank out email addresses and licence keys, and tell Sentry not to record your IP address. We do this because a failed payment or a broken page has to be found and fixed.

What you send us

  • Call-back and download forms: what you type is sent to our email through Web3Forms. If you use WhatsApp instead, WhatsApp handles the message. We use it only to answer you.
  • Buying Pro: PayPal takes the payment and we never see your card details. We keep your email address, licence key, plan, amount, payment reference and the random install ids your key is used with, to give you your key, check it, help you and handle refunds. The key is emailed through Resend, and we get a short email about the sale without your email address or key.

Other services the site uses

To show a page, your browser also contacts GitHub (hosts the site), Google Fonts (the lettering) and, on buy pages, PayPal and our licence server, which runs on Cloudflare. Like any web server they see your IP address.

Your control

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email info@zenitherps.com. We keep payment records only as long as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top.
