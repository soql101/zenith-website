Full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.

What happens next

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are at our discretion. If something does not work, email us first: we would rather fix it.

Delivery

Licence keys are digital. Your key is shown on the page straight after payment and emailed to you. Nothing is shipped.
