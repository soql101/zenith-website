Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Full refund within 14 days of buying. No questions asked.

This policy covers Pro licences for Zenith Chrome extensions, such as Zenith Account Switcher Pro and Zenith Timesheet Pro, bought on zenitherps.com.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email info@zenitherps.com within 14 days of your payment.
  2. Include your licence key, or the email address you paid with.

You do not need to give a reason. We reply within 2 working days.

What happens next

  • We refund the full amount you paid through PayPal, back to the account or card you paid with.
  • PayPal usually shows the refund straight away. A card refund can take 5 to 10 working days to reach your statement, depending on your bank.
  • Your licence key is switched off. The extension goes back to the free version at its next check. Your saved data stays in your browser.

After 14 days

We do not normally refund after 14 days. If the extension stops working for you because of a fault we cannot fix, email us and we will look at it fairly.

Paid by UPI or bank transfer

The same 14-day rule applies. We send the refund back to the same UPI ID or bank account.

Cancellation

Nothing renews. Every licence is a one-time payment, including the one-year plan, so there is no subscription to cancel. A one-year licence simply ends after a year unless you buy again.

Disputes

Please email us before opening a dispute or chargeback with PayPal or your bank. A refund from us is faster. If a payment is disputed or reversed, the licence key is switched off.
