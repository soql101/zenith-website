Zenith Timesheet

Your calendar already knows how many hours you worked. Zenith Timesheet reads the events in your Google Calendar™ and adds up the hours per client, for any period you choose, so you can attach a clean timesheet to your invoice.

See Pro prices Email Us

How it works

  1. Install the extension and press Connect Google Calendar. Access is read-only.
  2. Pick a period: this week, last week, this month, last month or any dates.
  3. Tell it which events belong to which client: by calendar, colour, a word in the title or a guest's email domain.
  4. Check the report and export it to CSV, Excel or PDF.

Who it is for

People who bill by the hour: consultants, freelancers, lawyers, accountants, tutors, coaches and small studios.

Which Google data it uses, and why

  • Your list of calendars (read-only), so you can choose which calendars count.
  • Your events (read-only): title, start and end, status, type, colour and guests' email addresses, so each event can be matched to a client and its hours added up.

It cannot change or delete anything in your calendar. Your events are processed in your own browser and are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no ads and no analytics in the extension. Read the full privacy policy.

Free and Pro

  • Free: hours for any period, up to 3 clients, matching by calendar and colour, CSV export for up to 31 days.
  • Pro: unlimited clients, matching by title word and guest email domain, Excel and PDF export, CSV for any range, hourly rates and amounts, rounding and minimum rules, overlap options, saved reports, your name and logo on the PDF.
