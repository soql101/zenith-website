Questions and answers

Which websites does it work on?

Most websites that keep you logged in with cookies: social media, online shops and seller dashboards, ad managers, project tools, forums and most other web apps.

Does it work with Google or Microsoft accounts?

No. Google accounts (Gmail, YouTube, Google Drive) and Microsoft accounts (Outlook, Office 365, Teams) protect their logins in a way that stops them switching reliably. Use Google's or Microsoft's own account switcher, or a separate Chrome profile, for those. Logins made with "Continue with Google", "Sign in with Apple" or similar buttons, and some banking sites, may also not switch correctly.

Can I use two accounts side by side at the same time?

One account is active per website at a time. To use a second account next to the first, open it in an incognito window or in another Chrome profile.

Where are my saved logins kept? Is it safe?

Only on your own computer, encrypted. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and there is no sign-up and no cloud. You can also lock everything with a passphrase.

Does it see or store my passwords?

No. It saves the website's login session (its cookies), never your password.

What if I forget my passphrase?

It cannot be recovered, not even by us. Delete the saved logins in the extension's settings, set a new passphrase and save your accounts again.

A saved account stopped working. What do I do?

Saved logins can expire, just like normal ones. Log in to that account again on the website, then press "Update saved login" in the extension.

Is it allowed to use several accounts on one website?

That depends on the website. Some allow only one account per person, so please follow the rules of each site you use. The extension does not hide your identity or get round any website's limits.

Which browsers does it work in?

It is made for Google Chrome on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS. Other browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store, such as Microsoft Edge or Brave, may work but are not tested.

What do I get free, and what does Pro add?

Free: up to 5 saved accounts, the passphrase lock and all 11 languages. Pro: unlimited saved accounts, client folders and search, notes, keyboard shortcuts and an encrypted backup file.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment for a lifetime licence. Nothing renews and you are never charged again.

How many browsers can I use my key on?

Up to 3 of your own browsers, for example your work laptop, your home computer and a second Chrome profile.

How do I get my licence key?

It is shown on this page as soon as the payment goes through, and emailed to your PayPal email address. Can't find it? Check your spam folder, or email info@zenitherps.com with the email address you paid with.

How do I move to a new computer?

Install the extension and enter the same licence key. Saved logins stay on the old computer: with Pro, make an encrypted backup file there and open it on the new one.

Can I pay from outside the US, or from India?

Yes. Prices are in US dollars and PayPal converts them to your currency (your bank or PayPal may charge a small conversion fee). PayPal cannot take payments between two Indian accounts, so from India WhatsApp us to pay by UPI.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. See the refund policy.
